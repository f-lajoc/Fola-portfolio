A little about me

I'm a Nigerian writer who found her way from rock samples to rich storytelling. With a B.Sc. in Geology from Federal University Lokoja, I spent a year as an environmental geologist during NYSC, mapping terrain and writing field reports. Then I turned fully toward words.

Today I write SEO and AEO-optimised B2B articles, run a growing entertainment brand called Life of Kunmi, and report features for a women's digital publication. I've also ghostwritten a 100,000-word novel, written YouTube scripts and handled sales calls, so I'm comfortable wherever the brief goes.
